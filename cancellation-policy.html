Cancellation Policy

If you choose to cancel your lease early, the following payments will be required immediately upon submitting a cancellation request:

  • Any outstanding rent payments (past-due amounts).
  • Rent for one rent cycle following the cancellation request.
  • A penalty equivalent to one rent cycle.

If your payment plan is biweekly, the following will apply:

  • Two rent cycles will be owed for the upcoming billing period.
  • A penalty will be charged that is equivalent to two rent cycles.

You may choose to apply your last month's rent deposit toward the cancellation amounts. If not applied, our standard deposit refund policy and schedule will apply.

Note: In the event of a cancellation, the lease will terminate at the end of the next rent cycle.

Service Fee

The service fee covers third-party online services, including applications, lease generation, and related transactions. This fee is non-refundable, regardless of any deposit or rent refunds or the timing of the cancellation request.

Early Termination Request

For early termination requests, please email info@circlestay.ca with your booking reference.
