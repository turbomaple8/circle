Sherbourne & Bloor

The Sherbourne is a modern high-rise rental at 561 Sherbourne Street, close to Sherbourne and Bloor, with more than 400 suites. Sherbourne Station on Line 2 is about a five-minute walk, Yorkville and the Bloor Street shops are close by, and both Toronto Metropolitan University and the University of Toronto are within walking distance. Residents share a concierge, fitness centre, yoga area, theatre room, games room, party room, lounge and library, and an outdoor terrace.
